1. IMPORTANT NOTE ABOUT EDIT MODE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Many wrestlers in this hack use special rules that are NOT visible in Edit
Mode. They are built into the game code: custom entrance gear, capes with
physics, themes per attire, custom call names and more.

These rules are tied to the wrestler ID, to the costume number (1-4), or to
specific attire/model numbers. If you change Entrance Attire, Upper Attire
(shirts/jackets), Ring Attire or the theme of these wrestlers in Edit Mode,
you may:
  - lose parts of the clothing or see clothing doubled/clipping through
    (e.g. a shirt showing through the entrance jacket),
  - get the "wrong" theme or lose a theme,
  - get entrance gear that does not belong to that wrestler.

I can NOT guarantee that modified costumes will work without bugs.
Recommendation: do not change Entrance/Upper/Ring Attire or themes of the
pre-made wrestlers. Your own Edit wrestlers are fine, as long as you don't
combine shirts with entrance jackets (see 3.6).

3. WHAT DEPENDS ON WHAT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Costume number vs. costume content
    Every wrestler has 4 costumes (Attire 1-4). Most special rules look at
    the costume NUMBER, not at what is set inside it. For example, if you
    completely rebuild Misawa's costume 3, he still gets the Tiger Mask II
    theme.

3.2 Themes per attire
    A built-in table "wrestler + costume number -> theme".
    It takes priority over the theme set in Edit Mode.
      Hulk Hogan      Attire 3 -> Hogan WCW,  Attire 4 -> Hogan NJPW
      Sting           Attire 1 -> Sting Crow
      Randy Savage    Attire 2 + 3 -> Savage WCW
      Ric Flair       Attire 1 + 2 -> Flair WCW
      K. Yamazaki     Attire 2 -> Tiger Mask (IV)
      M. Misawa       Attire 3 -> Tiger Mask II
      K. Kanemoto     Attire 3 -> Tiger Mask III
    Consequences:
      - Changing the theme in Edit Mode has NO effect on these attires.
      - The other attires of these wrestlers use their normal theme.
      - The Tiger Mask call name is tied to the theme: only the three
        Tiger Mask attires above are announced as "Tiger Mask".
      - The DomeRoad light show works with all themes, including these.
    Tiger Mask's own theme (all attires) sits in the former "Triple Crown"
    slot of the theme list.

3.3 Upper Attire as entrance gear (Hase, Takayama, Omori, Ace, Barton)
    Costumes 1-3 have Entrance Attire "9" set. For THESE wrestlers, 9 means:
    "wear the Upper Attire from costume 4 during the entrance and take it off
    for the match".
      - Changing the Entrance Attire of costumes 1-3 -> the jacket/shirt is
        missing during the entrance.
      - Changing the Upper Attire of costume 4 -> also changes the entrance
        gear of costumes 1-3.
      - For all other wrestlers, Entrance 9 is simply the normal Entrance
        Attire A9.

3.4 Scott Steiner (nWo) and Scott Hall
      - Steiner nWo: the entrance hood is tied to the wrestler ID and appears
        with all costumes, regardless of the Entrance Attire setting.
      - Scott Hall: the entrance gear depends on the RING Attire (not on the
        costume number): Ring Attire A9 -> vest, A6 -> Outsiders shirt.
        Changing the Ring Attire = different or no entrance gear.

3.5 Capes with physics (Tiger Mask, Misawa, Kanemoto, Ultimo Dragon, Eddie)
    All these capes use the same two cape models. The physics are tied to
    exactly these models (modelled after Liger's cape).
      - Colors/textures of the capes can differ, the shape is the same for
        all of them.
      - Giving these clothing parts to other wrestlers or combining them with
        other Entrance Attires has not been tested.

3.6 Entrance Attire + shirts (Upper Attire)
    Several Entrance Attires were rebuilt for specific wrestlers (different
    models than in the original game, e.g. the Hayabusa robe B7 uses the
    coat shape of B5/B6). An Entrance Attire only replaces certain body
    parts - everything else stays visible. Therefore:
      - Shirt (Upper Attire) + entrance jacket can cause the shirt to show
        through or parts of the jacket to be missing.
      - Safe: Entrance Attire without a shirt, or a shirt without an
        Entrance Attire.
    Entrance Attire numbers only go up to 31 (the game cannot store more).

3.7 Masks
    Mask 20 (Vader) has its own head model and a round face. A different
    face shape/body size may look different with this mask than with normal
    masks.

4. TECHNICAL INFO / FLASHCARTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - ROM size approx. 63.7 MB (fits within 64 MB).
  - Made for real hardware. SummerCart64: should work (save type SRAM
    256 kbit, detected via the game ID - otherwise set it manually in the
    cart menu).
  - NTSC (60 Hz). Japanese and US consoles: no problem.
    PAL consoles (Europe): picture only with a 60 Hz capable TV or a
    modified console.
  - Old save files from other VPW2 versions may contain settings that mean
    something different here - it's best to start with a fresh save.
